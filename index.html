About

Hello! I'm Jolene, a Software Engineering and Business Information Management student at UC Irvine interested in the intersection of technology, business, and people.

I really enjoy understanding how things work, whether that means debugging a program, investigating an unexpected system alert, or thinking about how a product could be easier for someone to use. Through my coursework and projects, I've explored areas including software development, databases, and user-centered design. I also work with UCI's Office of Information Technology, where I help monitor university applications and systems and investigate incidents when something goes wrong.

I'm especially interested in opportunities where I can keep building my technical skills while working on technology that solves real problems. Right now, I'm exploring software engineering, AI, and the broader intersection between technical development and product.

Outside of tech, you'll probably find me learning K-pop choreography, discovering new music, or trying something new around Orange County!
